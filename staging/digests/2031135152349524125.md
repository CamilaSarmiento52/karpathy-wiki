## Summary
A single X post on the first round of autoresearch on nanochat (#1). He says he left it
tuning a depth=12 model for about two days, that it found about 20 changes that improved
validation loss, and that when he tested them all were additive and transferred to depth=24;
stacked, they took the leaderboard's "Time to GPT-2" from 2.02 to 1.80 hours (#1). He calls it
a first for him, after two decades of doing this optimization by hand, and lists the bigger
findings, including oversights in his own code (#1). He says it is not novel research yet but
the adjustments are real (#1). He predicts all LLM frontier labs will do this, through swarms
of agents tuning small models and promoting ideas to larger scales, and says any metric that
is reasonably efficient to evaluate, or has an efficient proxy, can be autoresearched (#1).
