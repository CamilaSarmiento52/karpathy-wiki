# nanochat now trains GPT-2 capability model in just 2 hours on a single 8XH100…
Raw: [[raw/x/2029701092347630069]] · Original: https://x.com/karpathy/status/2029701092347630069

One post, so no locations are given.

## Summary
He says nanochat now trains a GPT-2 capability model in 2 hours on one 8×H100 node, down in his words from about 3 hours a month before, which brings it closer to interactive. Tuning and features such as fp8 contributed, but the biggest difference was switching the dataset from FineWeb-edu to NVIDIA's ClimbMix; Olmo, FineWeb and DCLM had all regressed, and ClimbMix worked so well out of the box that he is slightly suspicious of goodharting, though the paper reads as about okay to him. He also has AI agents iterating on nanochat automatically: on a feature branch an agent tries ideas, merges those that work and iterates, 110 changes in about 12 hours that took a d12's validation loss from 0.862415 to 0.858039 at no wall-clock cost. Over about two weeks, he says, he has iterated more on this agent "meta-setup" than on nanochat itself.
