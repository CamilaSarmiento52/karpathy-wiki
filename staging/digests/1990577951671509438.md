# I'm starting to get into a habit of reading everything with LLMs
Raw: [[raw/x/1990577951671509438]] · Original: https://x.com/karpathy/status/1990577951671509438

## Summary
He says he is getting into the habit of reading everything, blogs, articles and book chapters, with LLMs, and that it is growing to be among his top use cases (#1). His usual process is three passes: first manual, then asking the LLM to explain or summarise, then Q&A, and he usually ends up understanding better and more deeply than if he had moved on (#1). He adds that writers may increasingly write for an LLM rather than for another human, because once an LLM understands an idea it can target, personalise and serve it to its user (#1).
