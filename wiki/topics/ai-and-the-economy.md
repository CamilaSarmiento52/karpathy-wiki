# AI and the economy
How he expects AI to enter the economy: as a continuation of computing and of centuries of automation, diffusing gradually into the same GDP exponential rather than producing a jump ([[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @1:07:55, @1:22:43, @1:28:11).

## What he says
**Definitions.** Asked how to chart progress toward AGI, he is tempted to reject the question, because he sees AI as an extension of computing, which no one charts on a single y-axis ([[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @1:07:55). He keeps the definition from when OpenAI started: a system that can do any economically valuable task at human performance or better ([[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @1:07:55, @1:08:32). Dropping physical work, as people commonly do, is a major concession; his guess, without the numbers, is that knowledge work is about 10% to 20% of the economy, still a few trillion dollars in the US ([[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @1:08:32, @1:09:16). Society will refactor jobs around which of their tasks are automatable ([[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @1:09:16).

**Coding first.** Asked why API revenue is dominated by coding, he says he believes coding is the perfect first domain for LLMs and agents, because it has always worked around text, and LLMs are text processors ([[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @1:14:46). On Dwarkesh's point that other text tasks are also hard, he says code is structured while text has more entropy, and that he does not have a very good answer ([[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @1:17:52).

**Automation, extended.** He sees superintelligence as an extrapolation of a gradual automation of society: more and more autonomous entities doing digital work, and physical work some amount of time later; it will be fundamentally automation but extremely foreign ([[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @1:18:29, @1:19:39). The outcome he thinks most likely is [[gradual-loss-of-control-is-the-most-likely-outcome]] ([[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @1:19:39).

**Already in an intelligence explosion.** He says we have been in an intelligence explosion for decades: the GDP curve is an exponential weighted sum over automation, from the Industrial Revolution to compilers ([[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @1:22:43). He tried to find AI in the GDP curve and could not, just as he could not find computers or mobile phones; technologies diffuse so slowly that they average into the same exponential ([[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @1:23:21, @1:23:57, @1:25:44). Asked by Dwarkesh whether that means growth stays at about 2%, he says yes, his expectation is the same pattern ([[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @1:26:19). He rejects the assumption of "God in a box": AI will do some things, fail at others and be put into society gradually; see [[ai-will-not-show-up-as-a-jump-in-gdp]] ([[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @1:28:11). The arguments for a jump in growth, billions of AI minds and population growth, are Dwarkesh's; he says he understands the population argument but does not intuitively feel it ([[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @1:27:00, @1:32:06).

**Bits and atoms.** Granting Dwarkesh's point about the cost of serving copies of a model, he says bits are a million times easier than anything that touches the physical world, so he would expect much faster adaptation in digital industry ([[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @1:52:56, @1:53:31).

**Compute.** He does not know that compute is being overbuilt: Claude Code and OpenAI Codex did not exist a year earlier, he believes, checking with Dwarkesh, and he expects demand to absorb what is being built ([[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @1:56:09).

**Note:** the slow diffusion he describes here is about GDP; his essay on individuals gaining more from LLMs than institutions is on [[technology-diffusion]] ([[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @1:23:57).
