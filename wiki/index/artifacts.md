# Artifacts

His repositories and what each demonstrates. Alphabetical. Maintained by ingest; do not edit by hand.

- [[build-nanogpt|build-nanogpt]] — the GPT-2 (124M) lecture's code, built commit by commit from an empty file; about an hour and $10 to reproduce
- [[calorie|calorie]] — his calorie-deficit tracking web app in one index.html
- [[llama2c|llama2.c]] — Llama 2 architecture trained in PyTorch and run from one dependency-free C file; tiny TinyStories models and int8 quantization
- [[llm-c|llm.c]] — GPT-2 training in C/CUDA; Discussions host his progress reports
- [[llm101n|LLM101n]] — his planned course on building a storyteller LLM end to end; archived until Eureka Labs has it ready
- [[makemore|makemore]] — character-level name generator built across the makemore lectures, from a bigram table to a 76k-parameter WaveNet-like model; one hackable file
- [[micrograd|micrograd]] — ~150-line scalar autograd engine and neural-net library
- [[minbpe|minbpe]] — his minimal, clean byte-level BPE tokenizer in Python, with training code tiktoken lacks and an exercise progression
- [[mingpt|minGPT]] — his small, clean, educational GPT re-implementation, semi-archived in favour of nanoGPT
- [[nanochat|nanochat]] — train and talk to your own GPT-2-grade model for about $48
- [[nanogpt|nanoGPT]] — his rewrite of minGPT with teeth, two ~300-line files that reproduce GPT-2 (124M); deprecated in favour of nanochat
- [[ng-video-lecture|ng-video-lecture]] — the GPT lecture's code, from a 120-line bigram script to a 200-line Transformer, released with its git log; initialisation left for a follow-up
- [[nn-zero-to-hero|nn-zero-to-hero]] — Neural Networks: Zero to Hero, his from-the-basics video course and its notebooks; eight lectures, ongoing
- [[rendergit|rendergit]] — a repo as one static HTML page; Human View and LLM View; vibe coded, not maintained
