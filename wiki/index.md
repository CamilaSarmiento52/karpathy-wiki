- [[claims|Claims]] — Specific positions he takes, dated, with a short quote.

- [[methods|Methods]] — How he builds, debugs, explains and evaluates.
- [[timelines|Timelines]] — How a view of his changed over time.
- [[principles|Principles]] — Rules extracted from several sources (phase 4).
- [[syntheses|Syntheses]] — Saved answers to questions; never cited as evidence.
