# LLM agents over time
- **2023-05-25** — Recommends low-stakes uses with human oversight, as copilots rather than autonomous agents; AutoGPT does not work well yet → [[use-llms-as-copilots-with-human-oversight]] ([[talk-2023-05-25-state-of-gpt]] @29:28, @30:02, @39:24, @39:57)
- **2024-03-26** — LLM systems are quite autonomous but not fully; oversight and evaluation will take the next few years to work out → [[llm-products-will-take-years-to-figure-out]] ([[talk-2024-03-26-sequoia-making-ai-accessible]] @06:54)
- **2024-09-05** — Self-driving reached AGI "a little bit" a decade after a near-perfect demo, yet has not spread across the world; he suspects AGI will look similar. He says AGI here, not agents → [[there-is-a-massive-gap-between-demo-and-product]] ([[talk-2024-09-05-no-priors-ep-80]] @01:07, @01:39, @02:11)
- **2025-06-19** — Calls this the decade of agents, not the year; agents need humans in the loop, as self-driving still does → [[this-is-the-decade-of-agents]] ([[youtube-2025-06-19-software-is-changing]] @27:09, @27:44)
- **2025-10-17** — In the Dwarkesh interview he restates the decade of agents: Claude and Codex are extremely impressive and used daily, but lack intelligence, multimodality, computer use and continual learning; going from demo to product is a march of nines; and the Atari-RL and Universe efforts went after agents too early → [[this-is-the-decade-of-agents]], [[there-is-a-massive-gap-between-demo-and-product]], [[early-agent-attempts-came-too-early]] ([[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @01:25, @01:59, @06:58, @1:45:36)
- **2025-12-19** — Names Claude Code the first convincing demonstration of an LLM agent, running on the developer's own computer → [[claude-code-is-the-first-convincing-llm-agent]] ([[blog-2025-12-19-year-in-review-2025]] §4)
- **2026-03-05** — Has AI agents iterating on nanochat automatically on a feature branch, merging ideas that work; reports the agents' 110 changes in about 12 hours lowering a d12's validation loss, and jokes about enjoying "the feeling of post-agi :)" → [[nanochat]], [[quick-experiment-loop]] ([[x-2026-03-05-nanochat-gpt-2-in-two-hours]])
- **2026-03-09** — Reports an agent doing his neural-net tuning workflow end to end and by itself, through about 700 changes, calls it a first for him, and predicts swarms of agents with humans "(optionally)" on the edges → [[autoresearch-found-real-improvements-to-nanochat]], [[all-frontier-labs-will-autoresearch]] ([[x-2026-03-09-autoresearch-tuning-nanochat]] #1)
- **2026-03-20** — Describes running several agents in parallel on macro actions, describes claws as a persistent layer above agents, and wants himself out of the loop wherever results can be evaluated; still says the whole thing does not fully work and going too far ahead is net not useful → [[take-yourself-out-of-the-agent-loop]], [[claws]] ([[talk-2026-03-20-skill-issue]] @04:25, @07:07, @16:28, @24:02)

**Note:** the dates are publication dates. Across these six sources the caution about autonomy is kept and a horizon, reasons, a mechanism and a working example are added; none of them withdraws the 2023-05-25 caution. The 2025-10-17 interview calls the agents he uses elementary-grade students while impressive, and the 2025-12-19 review calls Claude Code the first convincing demonstration of an agent; both can hold, so no contradiction is set. That reads as a view that accumulated rather than changed, an editorial reading of the sequence, not something he states ([[talk-2023-05-25-state-of-gpt]] @39:57; [[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @01:25, @1:43:07; [[blog-2025-12-19-year-in-review-2025]] §4).

**Note:** the 2026-03-05 and 2026-03-09 entries are agents running training experiments, autoresearch in the 2026-03-09 post: an agent editing training code against a validation metric, not RL on the model. They add a narrow task run without a human in the loop; neither post gives a horizon or mentions the decade of agents, so [[this-is-the-decade-of-agents]] is not revised ([[x-2026-03-05-nanochat-gpt-2-in-two-hours]]; [[x-2026-03-09-autoresearch-tuning-nanochat]] #1; [[youtube-2025-06-19-software-is-changing]] @27:09).

**Note:** the 2026-03-20 interview shifts the emphasis: in 2025 his response to being the bottleneck was small, verified diffs and humans in the loop; now it is to remove himself where results can be evaluated automatically, as autoresearch does, which there too is an agent editing training code against an objective metric, not RL on the model ([[youtube-2025-06-19-software-is-changing]] @23:48, @27:44; [[talk-2026-03-20-skill-issue]] @16:28, @17:32). He scopes this to evaluable tasks and says agents still do not fully work, and he gives no timescale, so nothing confirms or revises [[this-is-the-decade-of-agents]] ([[talk-2026-03-20-skill-issue]] @24:02, @28:24).
