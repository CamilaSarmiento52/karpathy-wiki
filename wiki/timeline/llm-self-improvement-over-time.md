# LLM self-improvement over time
- **2023-11-23** — In Intro to LLMs he says LLMs only do AlphaGo's first stage, imitation, and that self-improvement may be possible in narrow domains with a reward function: [[self-improvement-may-be-possible-in-narrow-domains]] ([[youtube-2023-11-23-intro-to-llms]] @39:20, @40:27). In the same talk LLMs "currently" have only a System 1 and cannot convert time into accuracy: [[llms-currently-only-have-a-system-1]] ([[youtube-2023-11-23-intro-to-llms]] @36:31, @37:06).
- **2024-03-26** — In the Sequoia interview he says the field has done AlphaGo's imitation step but not its RL step, that the model must practise for itself, and that RLHF does not even count as RL, he thinks: [[rlhf-is-not-real-rl]] ([[talk-2024-03-26-sequoia-making-ai-accessible]] @24:59, @25:32, @26:04, @26:39).
- **2025-02-05** — In the Deep Dive lecture he describes RL on practice problems with known answers, with DeepSeek-R1 and OpenAI's thinking models as examples, in which models discover their own chains of thought: [[rl-lets-models-discover-their-own-thinking-strategies]] ([[youtube-2025-02-05-deep-dive-into-llms]] @2:28:46, @2:32:04). He says LLMs show only hints of AlphaGo's curve so far ([[youtube-2025-02-05-deep-dive-into-llms]] @2:44:20, @2:44:52), and repeats that RLHF is not RL in that sense ([[youtube-2025-02-05-deep-dive-into-llms]] @3:05:54, @3:06:27).
- **2025-10-13** — In the nanochat launch post he gives "reinforcement learning is sucking supervision bits through a straw" as the reason the GSM8K RL stage runs for about 1.5 hours: [[rl-sucks-supervision-bits-through-a-straw]] ([[github-2025-10-13-nanochat-discussion-introducing]] §Reinforcement Learning (RL)).
- **2025-10-17** — In the Dwarkesh interview he calls RL terrible but better than the imitation before it, because one final reward is broadcast across a whole trajectory; LLM judges used as rewards are gamed within tens of steps; and LLMs have no equivalent yet of culture or self-play: [[rl-is-terrible-but-better-than-imitation]], [[llm-judges-are-gameable-rewards]], [[llms-lack-culture-and-self-play]] ([[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @41:33, @42:46, @47:11, @1:41:47).

**Note:** what changed between 2023-11-23 and 2025-02-05 is that the narrow-domain RL he speculated about and the time-into-accuracy wish both appear in his account as working thinking models, so the 2025 claim extends both 2023 claims; what did not change is his framing of it as early, short of AlphaGo, and his view of RLHF as a weak form of RL ([[youtube-2023-11-23-intro-to-llms]] @40:27; [[talk-2024-03-26-sequoia-making-ai-accessible]] @26:39; [[youtube-2025-02-05-deep-dive-into-llms]] @2:44:20, @3:05:54).

**Note:** the 2025-10-13 and 2025-10-17 entries add to the sequence without withdrawing anything. RL is credited with hill-climbing on problems with correct answers and in the same breath called noisy and still stupid; the 2024-03-26 ranking of imitation below RL is kept; and he expects three to five more ideas to be needed. On what these sources allow, his view accumulated rather than changed, and none of them says self-improvement has arrived ([[github-2025-10-13-nanochat-discussion-introducing]] §Reinforcement Learning (RL); [[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @41:33, @44:36, @45:19; [[talk-2024-03-26-sequoia-making-ai-accessible]] @27:13).
