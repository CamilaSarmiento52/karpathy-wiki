# LLM self-improvement over time
- **2023-11-23** — In Intro to LLMs he says LLMs only do AlphaGo's first stage, imitation, and that self-improvement may be possible in narrow domains with a reward function: [[self-improvement-may-be-possible-in-narrow-domains]] ([[youtube-2023-11-23-intro-to-llms]] @39:20, @40:27). In the same talk LLMs "currently" have only a System 1 and cannot convert time into accuracy: [[llms-currently-only-have-a-system-1]] ([[youtube-2023-11-23-intro-to-llms]] @36:31, @37:06).
- **2024-03-26** — In the Sequoia interview he says the field has done AlphaGo's imitation step but not its RL step, that the model must practise for itself, and that RLHF does not even count as RL, he thinks: [[rlhf-is-not-real-rl]] ([[talk-2024-03-26-sequoia-making-ai-accessible]] @24:59, @25:32, @26:04, @26:39).
- **2025-02-05** — In the Deep Dive lecture he describes RL on practice problems with known answers, with DeepSeek-R1 as his worked example and OpenAI's o1/o3-mini, which he says were trained similarly per OpenAI employees' public statements, in which models discover their own chains of thought: [[rl-lets-models-discover-their-own-thinking-strategies]] ([[youtube-2025-02-05-deep-dive-into-llms]] @2:28:46, @2:32:04, @2:37:07). He says LLMs show only hints of AlphaGo's curve so far ([[youtube-2025-02-05-deep-dive-into-llms]] @2:44:20, @2:44:52), and repeats that RLHF is not RL in that sense ([[youtube-2025-02-05-deep-dive-into-llms]] @3:05:54, @3:06:27).
- **2025-10-13** — In the nanochat launch post he gives "reinforcement learning is sucking supervision bits through a straw" as the reason the GSM8K RL stage runs for about 1.5 hours: [[rl-sucks-supervision-bits-through-a-straw]] ([[github-2025-10-13-nanochat-discussion-introducing]] §Reinforcement Learning (RL)).
- **2025-10-17** — In the Dwarkesh interview he calls RL terrible, because one final reward is broadcast across a whole trajectory, but better than the imitation before it; LLM judges used as rewards get gamed, so RL against them works for only tens of steps; and LLMs have no equivalent yet of culture or self-play: [[rl-is-terrible-but-better-than-imitation]], [[llm-judges-are-gameable-rewards]], [[llms-lack-culture-and-self-play]] ([[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @41:33, @42:46, @46:34, @47:11, @1:41:47).
- **2026-03-09** — He reports his first autoresearch round, an agent that tuned nanochat's training code for about two days and found changes he measured as real, and predicts all frontier labs will do this, with swarms of agents promoting ideas from small models to larger ones: [[autoresearch-found-real-improvements-to-nanochat]], [[all-frontier-labs-will-autoresearch]] ([[x-2026-03-09-autoresearch-tuning-nanochat]] #1).
- **2026-03-20** — In the Skill Issue interview he says he is fundamentally interested in recursive self-improvement, LLMs improving LLMs, which he thinks all the frontier labs are trying to do "roughly speaking"; his autoresearch loop found nanochat tunings he had missed, and it needs an objective metric: [[all-frontier-labs-will-autoresearch]], [[autoresearch-needs-an-objective-metric]] ([[talk-2026-03-20-skill-issue]] @17:32, @18:04, @18:36, @24:02).

**Note:** the dates are publication dates, and on what these sources allow his view accumulated rather than changed. On 2023-11-23 he speculated that RL against a reward could make self-improvement possible in narrow domains; on 2025-02-05 RL against checkable answers is, in his account, training thinking models whose longer outputs raise accuracy on math and code, yet he sees only "hints" of AlphaGo-style progress beyond human level, calls the RL "primordial", and still treats RLHF as a weak form of RL. The 2025-10-13 and 2025-10-17 entries add to the sequence without withdrawing anything: RL is credited with hill-climbing on problems with correct answers and in the same breath called noisy and still stupid, the 2024-03-26 ranking of imitation below RL is kept, and he expects three to five more ideas to be needed. None of these sources says self-improvement has arrived; this is an editorial reading of the sequence ([[youtube-2023-11-23-intro-to-llms]] @40:27; [[talk-2024-03-26-sequoia-making-ai-accessible]] @26:39, @27:13; [[youtube-2025-02-05-deep-dive-into-llms]] @2:30:24, @2:44:52, @3:05:54, @3:29:18; [[github-2025-10-13-nanochat-discussion-introducing]] §Reinforcement Learning (RL); [[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @41:33, @44:36, @45:19).

**Note:** autoresearch is an agent editing a model's training code and keeping changes that lower a validation metric, not RL on the model's own weights or behaviour; the 2026-03-09 post does not call it RL or self-improvement, so it is placed here as a dated entry, not as evidence for the RL claims above ([[x-2026-03-09-autoresearch-tuning-nanochat]] #1; [[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @41:33).

**Note:** on 2026-03-20 he does use the words recursive self-improvement, but the loop he runs is still autoresearch: an agent editing a model's training code against an objective metric, not RL on the model, and he does not say AlphaGo-style self-improvement has arrived ([[talk-2026-03-20-skill-issue]] @17:32, @24:02; [[youtube-2023-11-23-intro-to-llms]] @40:27).
