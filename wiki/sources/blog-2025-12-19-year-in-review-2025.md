# 2025 LLM Year in Review
Raw: [[raw/blog/year-in-review-2025]] · Original: https://karpathy.bearblog.dev/year-in-review-2025/

## Summary
A year-end essay in six numbered sections. RLVR became a new major training stage that took
compute from pretraining (§1). LLM intelligence is jagged, "ghosts" not animals, and he lost
trust in benchmarks (§2). Cursor revealed a distinct LLM app layer, summarising his YC talk
(§3). Claude Code showed what an agent looks like, running locally (§4). Vibe coding
crossed a capability threshold and now empowers professionals too (§5). Nano banana hints at
an LLM GUI (§6). He closes saying LLMs are both smarter and dumber than he expected (§TLDR).
