# Andrej Karpathy: Software Is Changing (Again)
Raw: [[raw/youtube/LCEmiRjPEtQ]] · Original: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LCEmiRjPEtQ

## Summary
A 39-minute talk to students entering the industry. He extends Software 1.0 (code) and 2.0
(weights) with Software 3.0, where prompts written in English program an LLM (@03:19). He
compares LLMs to utilities, fabs and, most of all, operating systems at a 1960s stage of
computing (@08:54, @11:05), and describes their psychology as "people spirits" with jagged
intelligence and no long-term memory (@14:24, @16:04). He argues for partial-autonomy apps
with a GUI and an autonomy slider, keeping the AI "on the leash" (@18:17, @22:43), retells
vibe coding and MenuGen (@29:22, @31:38), and closes on building infrastructure for agents
(@33:19).
