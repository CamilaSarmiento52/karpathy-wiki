# Chemical hygiene
Raw: [[raw/blog/chemical-hygiene]] · Original: https://karpathy.bearblog.dev/chemical-hygiene/

Everything here is his personal practice and opinion as of 2025-12-18. He says he is not a professional toxicologist or food scientist, calls the guide evolving, opinionated and a work in progress, and the wiki reports his choices, not medical or scientific facts (§intro (opening), (closing)). Studies and pages he links are theirs; the wiki does not restate their findings.

## Summary
He presents the post as a follow-up to his digital hygiene guide and as his evolving, opinionated guide to chemical hygiene, written up because he keeps ranting about it to friends (§intro (opening)). He calls water and air the easiest parts, with what he describes as concrete, high-confidence recommendations: he recommends reverse-osmosis drinking water with a remineralisation filter, a whole-home or shower filter, and air filtration, and avoiding combustion, fragrances and air fresheners in the home (§intro (Water), (Air)). He calls food the hardest category, because of deep supply chains he says have been efficiency-maxxed with little regard for public health, and gives the sourcing labels he looks for and the cooking rule he puts first, avoiding plastics, especially with heat (§intro (Food), (Food sourcing), (Cooking & preparation)). He prefers natural fibres and simple cleaning products, and says where he has not changed his own habits, among them a plastic toothbrush, plastic floss and fluoride toothpaste (§intro (Fabrics), (Cleaning supplies), (Dental hygiene)). He says all of this only decreases risk, cannot eliminate it, and blames US regulation for lagging behind the industry and behind Europe (§intro (Learn more), (TLDR), (closing)).
