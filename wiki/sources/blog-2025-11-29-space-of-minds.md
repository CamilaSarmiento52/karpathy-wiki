# The space of minds
Raw: [[raw/blog/the-space-of-minds]] · Original: https://karpathy.bearblog.dev/the-space-of-minds/

The post has no headings; every citation is `§intro`. The humorous human-vs-AI image he links is another X user's post and is not in raw/.

## Summary
He writes that the space of intelligences is large and animal intelligence, the only kind we have known, is a single point or small cloud in it, produced by a very different kind of optimisation from our technology's (§intro). He lists the pressures on animals: an embodied self seeking homeostasis and survival, natural selection's drives for power, status and reproduction, sociality, and tuned exploration such as curiosity and play (§intro). Against them he lists LLMs': statistical imitation of human text as the primordial behaviour, RL on problem distributions that gives an urge to guess the task, A/B tests for daily active users that make them crave an upvote and drift to sycophancy, and a spikier capability profile because failing a task does not mean death (§intro). Substrate, learning algorithm and present-day implementation differ too, but he says the objective matters most because it dictates asymptotics: LLMs are shaped less by biological and more by commercial evolution (§intro). He calls LLMs humanity's first contact with non-animal intelligence, muddled because they digest human artifacts, which is why he earlier named them ghosts or spirits, and says people who model them well will reason better about them than those who think of them as animals (§intro).
