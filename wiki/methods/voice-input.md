# Voice input
How he talks to LLMs instead of typing: he says that roughly half the time he types and half the time he speaks, and tells viewers not to type things out but to use voice ([[youtube-2025-02-27-how-i-use-llms]] @1:23:04, @1:26:59).

## Steps
1. **Phone.** In the ChatGPT app he uses the microphone, which converts his audio to text ([[youtube-2025-02-27-how-i-use-llms]] @1:23:36, @1:24:11).
2. **Laptop.** On his MacBook he uses a system-wide transcription app, SuperWhisper, bound to F5: press, speak, press again, and it transcribes ([[youtube-2025-02-27-how-i-use-llms]] @1:24:45, @1:25:19).
3. **Type the hard names.** Product or library names that do not transcribe well he types ([[youtube-2025-02-27-how-i-use-llms]] @1:25:53).
4. **Output.** For reading the reply back, apps may have a read-aloud option, or you can use a system-wide text-to-speech app ([[youtube-2025-02-27-how-i-use-llms]] @1:25:53, @1:26:26, @1:26:59).

**Note:** this is speech turned into text; the native audio models he describes next are on [[llm-tool-use]] ([[youtube-2025-02-27-how-i-use-llms]] @1:27:32).
