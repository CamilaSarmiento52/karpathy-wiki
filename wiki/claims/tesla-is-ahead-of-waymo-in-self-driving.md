# He thinks Tesla, with a software problem, is ahead of Waymo, with a hardware problem
> "I think software problems are much easier" — [[talk-2024-09-05-no-priors-ep-80]] @02:11

## Context
He says people think Waymo is ahead of Tesla, that he personally thinks Tesla is ahead, and that he is still very bullish on its self-driving program ([[talk-2024-09-05-no-priors-ep-80]] @02:11). His reason: Tesla has a software problem and Waymo a hardware problem, software problems are much easier, and Tesla already has cars deployed at scale that Waymo still needs ([[talk-2024-09-05-no-priors-ep-80]] @02:11). He drove the latest build the day before and calls some of its driving miraculous; Waymo looks like it is winning now, but he expects Tesla to be ahead in ten years ([[talk-2024-09-05-no-priors-ep-80]] @02:45, @03:17). On sensors, Tesla uses lidar and other expensive sensors at training time and distils the result into a vision-only test-time package, which he calls an underappreciated arbitrage ([[talk-2024-09-05-no-priors-ep-80]] @03:49).
