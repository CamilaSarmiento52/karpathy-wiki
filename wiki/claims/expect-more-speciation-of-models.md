# He expects more speciation of AI models, smaller specialised models instead of one oracle
> "I do think we should expect more speciation in the intelligences" — [[talk-2026-03-20-skill-issue]] @29:29

## Context
Asked by the host whether models should be unbundled into experts, he says his impression is that the labs are trying to have one monoculture model that is arbitrarily intelligent in every domain ([[talk-2026-03-20-skill-issue]] @29:29). As the animal kingdom has very diverse brains, he expects speciation: you do not need an oracle that knows everything, but much smaller models that keep the cognitive core and then specialise, becoming more efficient in latency or throughput on specific tasks; mathematicians working in Lean are his example of a domain targeted by some releases ([[talk-2026-03-20-skill-issue]] @29:29, @30:03). He thinks labs serve one model because they do not know what end users will ask, and that partnering with a business on specific problems might show more specialisation ([[talk-2026-03-20-skill-issue]] @31:41). Part of why speciation is not here yet, he says, is that the science of changing the weights, fine-tuning without losing capabilities or continual learning, is less developed than manipulating context windows, and it also has to be cheap enough ([[talk-2026-03-20-skill-issue]] @31:41, @32:14, @32:48). The remark at @31:08 that little speciation has been seen has an unclear speaker and is not attributed to him ([[talk-2026-03-20-skill-issue]] @31:08).
