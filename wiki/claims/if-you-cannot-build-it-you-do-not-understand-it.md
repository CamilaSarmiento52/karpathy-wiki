# He holds that building something from scratch is the only way to really understand it
> "Build the code, arrange it, get it to work." — [[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @30:04

## Context
Asked how best to learn from nanochat, about 8,000 lines in his spoken figure, he says to put it on the right monitor and build it from scratch, allowed to reference it but not to copy-paste ([[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @28:48, @29:27). Building from scratch, he says, forces you to come to terms with what you do not understand without knowing you do not understand it, and always leads to a deeper understanding ([[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @29:27, @30:04). He gives "If I can't build it, I don't understand it" as a Feynman quote, he believes, and says he has always believed it 100% ([[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @30:04). The line is Feynman's; the claim is that he holds it. Hence "don't write blog posts, don't do slides": build the code and get it to work, or you are missing knowledge ([[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @30:04).
