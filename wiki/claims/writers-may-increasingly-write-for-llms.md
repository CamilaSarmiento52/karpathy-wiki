# Writers may increasingly write for an LLM rather than for another human
> "it can then target, personalize and serve the idea to its user" — [[x-2025-11-18-reading-with-llms]] #1

## Context
Having described his habit of reading everything with LLMs, he turns to the flip side: for a writer trying to explain or communicate something, we may increasingly see less of a mindset of writing for another human and more of writing for an LLM ([[x-2025-11-18-reading-with-llms]] #1). His reason is that once an LLM "gets it", it can target, personalise and serve the idea to its user ([[x-2025-11-18-reading-with-llms]] #1). He hedges it with "may increasingly" ([[x-2025-11-18-reading-with-llms]] #1).
