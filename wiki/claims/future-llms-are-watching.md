> "Everything we do today might be scrutinized in great detail in the future" — [[blog-2025-12-10-auto-grade-hn]] §intro

Everything done today might be scrutinised in great detail in the future, he says, because doing so will be "free" ([[blog-2025-12-10-auto-grade-hn]] §intro).
