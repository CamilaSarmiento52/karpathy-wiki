# In April 2025 he judged vibe coding full web apps messy and not yet a good idea for anything important
> "not a good idea for anything of actual importance" — [[blog-2025-04-27-vibe-coding-menugen]] §intro ¶For now

## Context
Closing the MenuGen post, he says vibe coding full web apps today is kind of messy and not a good idea for anything of actual importance ([[blog-2025-04-27-vibe-coding-menugen]] §intro ¶For now). In the same paragraph he sees "clear hints of greatness" and thinks the industry just needs a bit of time to adapt to LLMs ([[blog-2025-04-27-vibe-coding-menugen]] §intro ¶For now). He describes himself as left with an equal mix of amazement, since it is possible and much faster than before, and frustration ([[blog-2025-04-27-vibe-coding-menugen]] §intro ¶Going forward). He hopes the barrier to building an app drops to about zero, so anyone could publish one as easily as making a TikTok ([[blog-2025-04-27-vibe-coding-menugen]] §intro ¶For now).

The judgement is "today": he wrote the post as someone with little to no web development experience, exploring how feasible vibe coding apps is at that point ([[blog-2025-04-27-vibe-coding-menugen]] §intro ¶3, ¶For now).
