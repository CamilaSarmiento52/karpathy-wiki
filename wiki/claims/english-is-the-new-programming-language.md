# English is now a programming language
> "we're now programming computers in English" — [[youtube-2025-06-19-software-is-changing]] @04:23

## Context
He made this point while presenting Software 3.0, calling it remarkable that the new paradigm runs in a native human language, and says it is his pinned tweet ([[youtube-2025-06-19-software-is-changing]] @03:51, @04:23). He returns to it to argue that everyone is now a programmer, where software used to take five to ten years of study ([[youtube-2025-06-19-software-is-changing]] @28:49, @29:22).

In December 2025 he says AI crossed the threshold for building impressive programs simply via English ([[blog-2025-12-19-year-in-review-2025]] §5).

At Sequoia Ascent (2026-04-29) he restates Software 3.0 as programming turning into prompting, with the context window as the lever over the LLM interpreter ([[talk-2026-04-29-sequoia-ascent-2026]] @03:18). His example is the OpenClaw installer: instead of a ballooning shell script, a block of text you give your agent, which looks at your environment and debugs in the loop; "That's the programming paradigm now." ([[talk-2026-04-29-sequoia-ascent-2026]] @03:50, @04:22).
