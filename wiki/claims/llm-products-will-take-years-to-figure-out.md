# Working out how to oversee and evaluate LLM products will take the next few years
> "it's quite autonomous but not fully autonomous" — [[talk-2024-03-26-sequoia-making-ai-accessible]] @06:54

## Context
Discussing where startups can build beside the big labs, he likens the moment to early iPhone apps, which were mostly jokes and took time to develop ([[talk-2024-03-26-sequoia-making-ai-accessible]] @06:20). People are still working out what LLMs are good and bad at, how to program and debug with them, how to get them to do real tasks, and, because they are quite autonomous but not fully, what oversight and evaluation should look like ([[talk-2024-03-26-sequoia-making-ai-accessible]] @06:20, @06:54). He expects that to take some time and to play out over the next few years ([[talk-2024-03-26-sequoia-making-ai-accessible]] @06:54). Earlier in the interview he describes the direction as bringing up and down relatively self-contained agents that can be given high-level tasks ([[talk-2024-03-26-sequoia-making-ai-accessible]] @04:39).
