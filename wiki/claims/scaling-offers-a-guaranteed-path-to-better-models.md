# In 2023 he said scaling offers one guaranteed path to better models, with algorithmic progress a bonus
> "algorithmic progress is not necessary it's a very nice bonus" — [[youtube-2023-11-23-intro-to-llms]] @26:02

## Context
Because next-word accuracy is a smooth, predictable function of parameters and training text and shows no sign of topping out, he says you can get more powerful models essentially for free by training a bigger model on more data ([[youtube-2023-11-23-intro-to-llms]] @26:02, @27:10). Organizations still invest a lot in algorithmic progress, but fundamentally, he says, scaling offers one guaranteed path to success, which is what drives the race for bigger GPU clusters and more data ([[youtube-2023-11-23-intro-to-llms]] @27:10, @27:43).
