# He endorses the line that you can outsource your thinking but not your understanding
> "the LLMs certainly don't excel at understanding" — [[talk-2026-04-29-sequoia-ascent-2026]] @28:59

## Context
Asked what remains worth learning deeply as intelligence gets cheap, he recalls a recent tweet, by someone he does not name, roughly that you can outsource your thinking but not your understanding, and says he thinks it is really nicely put ([[talk-2026-04-29-sequoia-ascent-2026]] @27:55). The line is the tweet's; the claim is that he endorses it ([[talk-2026-04-29-sequoia-ascent-2026]] @27:55). He says he is still part of the system, information still has to reach his brain, and he feels he is becoming a bottleneck in knowing what to build, why it is worth doing and how to direct his agents ([[talk-2026-04-29-sequoia-ascent-2026]] @27:55, @28:27). Something still has to direct the thinking and processing, and that is fundamentally constrained by understanding ([[talk-2026-04-29-sequoia-ascent-2026]] @28:27). Because LLMs do not excel at understanding, he says, you remain uniquely in charge of it, and tools that enhance understanding, such as his knowledge bases, excite him ([[talk-2026-04-29-sequoia-ascent-2026]] @28:27, @28:59).
