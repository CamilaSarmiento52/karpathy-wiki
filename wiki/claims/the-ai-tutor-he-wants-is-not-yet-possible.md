# At current capability, the AI tutor he wants cannot be built
> "At the current capability, you don't." — [[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @2:01:07

## Context
His bar is a one-on-one Korean tutor who quickly understood what he knew and did not know, probed his world model, and served material at the right level, so that he was the only constraint on his learning ([[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @1:59:51, @2:00:31, @2:01:07). No LLM does that for you right now, "not even close", he says ([[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @2:00:31). Asked how to automate it, he says that at current capability you don't, so it is not the right time to build that AI tutor; it is still a useful product others will build, and ChatGPT is already valuable for education ([[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @2:01:07). As a computer-vision consultant his value was often telling companies not to use AI, and he feels the same about education now ([[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @2:01:45). For now he is building something more conventional, with physical and digital components ([[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @2:02:21).
