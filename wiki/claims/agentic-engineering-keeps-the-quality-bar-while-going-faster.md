# Vibe coding raises the floor; agentic engineering keeps professional software's quality bar while going faster
> "agentic engineering is about preserving the quality bar" — [[talk-2026-04-29-sequoia-ascent-2026]] @15:49

## Context
Answering the host, who used the term "agentic engineering" first, he says vibe coding is about raising the floor for everyone, "everyone can vibe code anything, and that's amazing", while agentic engineering preserves the quality bar of professional software: no vulnerabilities introduced through vibe coding, and you are still responsible for your software as before ([[talk-2026-04-29-sequoia-ascent-2026]] @15:49). Whether you can go faster that way, he says, "spoiler is you can"; doing it properly means coordinating spiky, fallible, stochastic but extremely powerful agents without sacrificing the quality bar, which he calls an engineering discipline ([[talk-2026-04-29-sequoia-ascent-2026]] @16:22). He sees a very high ceiling, with people very good at it peaking at a lot more than 10x, from his perspective right now ([[talk-2026-04-29-sequoia-ascent-2026]] @16:55). He adopts and defines the term; the source does not show that he coined it ([[talk-2026-04-29-sequoia-ascent-2026]] @15:49, @16:22).
