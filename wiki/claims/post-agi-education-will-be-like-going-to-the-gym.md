# After AGI, he hopes education will be more like entertainment, effortful the way the gym is
> "learning something is kind of like going to the gym but for the brain" — [[talk-2024-09-05-no-priors-ep-80]] @40:29

## Context
He wants to make learning much easier ([[talk-2024-09-05-no-priors-ep-80]] @39:24). Before AGI people learn for practical reasons such as jobs; after AGI, he says, education becomes entertainment to a much larger extent ([[talk-2024-09-05-no-priors-ep-80]] @39:56). He likens learning to going to the gym for the brain: effortful, but a kind of fun with a payoff, which is the specific sense in which he means education is not fun ([[talk-2024-09-05-no-priors-ep-80]] @40:29, @41:01). What he would hope for after AGI is that people go to the gym a lot, mentally as well as physically, and that being highly educated is looked up to ([[talk-2024-09-05-no-priors-ep-80]] @41:01).

**Restated (Dwarkesh, published 2025-10-17).** Before AGI, he says, motivation is simple because people want to make money; he often says pre-AGI education is useful and "Post-AGI education is fun.", the way people still go to the gym although machines lift heavy objects: because it is fun, healthy and attractive ([[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @2:08:04, @2:08:41, @2:09:16). With a perfect tutor, he says, learning becomes trivial and desirable, and anyone will speak five languages ([[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @2:09:53, @2:10:30). In the long term he thinks it is a losing game, though a person can go much further than today ([[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @2:10:30). He expects a transitional period in which humans can stay in the loop if they understand a lot, which probably goes away in the long term, and knowing things might become a sport ([[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @2:13:10).
