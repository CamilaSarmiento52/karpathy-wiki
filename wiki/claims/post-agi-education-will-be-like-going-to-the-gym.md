# After AGI, he hopes education will be more like entertainment, effortful the way the gym is
> "learning something is kind of like going to the gym but for the brain" — [[talk-2024-09-05-no-priors-ep-80]] @40:29

## Context
He wants to make learning much easier ([[talk-2024-09-05-no-priors-ep-80]] @39:24). Before AGI people learn for practical reasons such as jobs; after AGI, he says, education becomes entertainment to a much larger extent ([[talk-2024-09-05-no-priors-ep-80]] @39:56). He likens learning to going to the gym for the brain: effortful, but a kind of fun with a payoff, which is the specific sense in which he means education is not fun ([[talk-2024-09-05-no-priors-ep-80]] @40:29, @41:01). What he would hope for after AGI is that people go to the gym a lot, mentally as well as physically, and that being highly educated is looked up to ([[talk-2024-09-05-no-priors-ep-80]] @41:01).
