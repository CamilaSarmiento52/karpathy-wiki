# Many jobs highly exposed to AI will be reshaped, not replaced
> "Many high-exposure jobs will be reshaped, not replaced." — [[github-2026-03-16-jobs-readme]] §LLM-powered coloring

## Context
The sentence is a caveat in the README of his [[jobs]] tool, under the heading on what its "AI Exposure" score is not: a high score does not predict that a job will disappear ([[github-2026-03-16-jobs-readme]] §LLM-powered coloring). Its example is software developers, whom the tool's LLM scores 9/10 because AI is transforming their work, while demand for software "could easily" grow as each developer becomes more productive ([[github-2026-03-16-jobs-readme]] §LLM-powered coloring). The README says the score leaves out demand elasticity, latent demand, regulatory barriers and social preferences for human workers, and that the scores are rough estimates from Gemini Flash via OpenRouter, not rigorous predictions ([[github-2026-03-16-jobs-readme]] §LLM-powered coloring). The claim is "many", stated as a caveat on the tool's own score, not a forecast argued at length ([[github-2026-03-16-jobs-readme]] §LLM-powered coloring).
