# He sees education as the hard technical problem of building ramps to knowledge
> "technical process of building ramps to knowledge." — [[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @2:03:02

## Context
He says people often think of education as a softer component of diffusing knowledge, while he has something hard and technical in mind: education is the very difficult technical process of building ramps to knowledge ([[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @2:03:02). nanochat, in his words, is a ramp to knowledge because it is very simple, and someone who looks through it learns a ton, a lot of what he calls "eurekas per second" ([[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @2:03:02, @2:03:38). He thinks of Eureka as maybe not that different from frontier-lab work: figuring out how to build such ramps efficiently, so that people are never stuck and nothing is too hard or too trivial ([[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @2:03:38).

Support, not extension: the same idea applied to a new project, neither denying the other.
