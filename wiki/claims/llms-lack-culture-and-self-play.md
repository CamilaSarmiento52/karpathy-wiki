# LLMs do not yet have an equivalent of culture or of self-play
> "LLMs don't really have culture right now" — [[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @1:40:36

## Context
Dwarkesh raises Quintin Pope's argument about human cultural accumulation; asked whether LLMs get that for free from training, he says "Yes and no": LLMs have no equivalent of culture, written record or passing notes to each other, which he calls one of the impediments ([[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @1:39:24, @1:40:36). The simplest case would be a giant scratchpad the LLM edits for itself, or an LLM writing a book that other LLMs read ([[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @1:40:36, @1:41:10). He names two powerful multi-agent ideas that have not really been claimed: culture, a growing repertoire of knowledge LLMs keep for their own purposes, and self-play, as AlphaGo played itself, for example one LLM making harder and harder problems for another to solve ([[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @1:41:10, @1:41:47). He has not seen either done convincingly; we are mostly in the single-agent realm, organisations of LLMs have not been seen convincingly either, and he expects that to change ([[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @1:41:47, @1:42:26).
