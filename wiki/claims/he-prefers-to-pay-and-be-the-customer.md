# He prefers to pay for products and be the customer, not the product
> "I prefer to be the customer, not the product in my digital life" — [[blog-2025-03-17-digital-hygiene]] §intro (Search engine)

## Context
**Digital hygiene, 2025-03-17.** He pays $3 a month for Brave Premium, as he says he does for all the services on his list, because he prefers to be the customer rather than the product ([[blog-2025-03-17-digital-hygiene]] §intro (Search engine)). Closing the post, he says he wants products that leak only the information they need, and wishes to pay for the software he uses so that incentives are aligned and he is the customer ([[blog-2025-03-17-digital-hygiene]] §intro (closing)). He admits trading sovereignty for convenience in places, still mostly using Gmail and X ([[blog-2025-03-17-digital-hygiene]] §intro (What's not on the list)).

**I love calculator, 2024-09-08.** The calculator post makes the ownership side of the same preference: you can buy a calculator in one single exchange for money, "You paid for it and now it is yours", and it has no other master ([[blog-2024-09-08-i-love-calculator]] §intro). Against that he asks, as a question, why companies would sell a product when they can rent it and monetise customer data ([[blog-2024-09-08-i-love-calculator]] §intro). **Note:** the calculator post does not say "customer" or "product" in this sense; it is a second source for paying and owning, not a restatement ([[blog-2024-09-08-i-love-calculator]] §intro; [[blog-2025-03-17-digital-hygiene]] §intro (closing)).
