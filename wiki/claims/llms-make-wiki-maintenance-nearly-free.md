# Humans abandon wikis because maintenance outgrows their value; LLMs make the maintenance nearly free
> "The wiki stays maintained because the cost of maintenance is near zero." — [[github-2026-04-04-llm-wiki-gist]] §Why this works

## Context
He says the tedious part of maintaining a knowledge base is not the reading or the thinking but the bookkeeping: updating cross-references, keeping summaries current, noting contradictions, keeping dozens of pages consistent ([[github-2026-04-04-llm-wiki-gist]] §Why this works). Humans abandon wikis because the maintenance burden grows faster than the value, while LLMs do not get bored, do not forget a cross-reference and can touch 15 files in one pass; the 15 is illustrative ([[github-2026-04-04-llm-wiki-gist]] §Why this works). He relates the idea in spirit to Vannevar Bush's Memex (1945) and says the part Bush could not solve was who does the maintenance, which the LLM handles ([[github-2026-04-04-llm-wiki-gist]] §Why this works). The human's job, in his framing, is to curate sources, direct the analysis, ask good questions and think about meaning; the LLM's is everything else ([[github-2026-04-04-llm-wiki-gist]] §Why this works).
