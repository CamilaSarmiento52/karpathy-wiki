# LLMs reverse the usual top-down diffusion of technology, so far benefiting individuals more than organisations
> "LLMs display a dramatic reversal of this pattern" — [[blog-2025-04-07-power-to-the-people]] §intro ¶2

## Context
**Power to the people, 2025-04-07.** The pattern he means is technology passing from governments or the military through corporations to individuals, as with electricity, computers, the internet and GPS ([[blog-2025-04-07-power-to-the-people]] §intro ¶1). LLMs, he says, generate disproportionate benefit for regular people, while their impact is more muted and lagging in corporations and governments ([[blog-2025-04-07-power-to-the-people]] §intro ¶2). He hedges it in time: at least for the moment and in aggregate across society, LLMs have been significantly more life-altering for individuals than for organisations, and he does not wish to downplay their impact on organisations ([[blog-2025-04-07-power-to-the-people]] §intro ¶4). Whether it lasts depends on whether money starts to buy dramatically better models, which he says would change things ([[blog-2025-04-07-power-to-the-people]] §intro ¶5). His three reasons are on [[technology-diffusion]] ([[blog-2025-04-07-power-to-the-people]] §intro ¶3, ¶4).

**YC talk, published 2025-06-19.** He says LLMs flip the direction of technology diffusion, with governments and corporations lagging behind everyone's adoption ([[youtube-2025-06-19-software-is-changing]] @12:45, @13:18). It is the same position, given as a second source, not a separate statement of a new view ([[youtube-2025-06-19-software-is-changing]] @13:18; [[blog-2025-04-07-power-to-the-people]] §intro ¶2).
