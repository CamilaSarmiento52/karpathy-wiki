# Today's frontier LLM research is not about building animals but summoning ghosts
> "It is about summoning ghosts." — [[blog-2025-10-01-animals-vs-ghosts]] §intro

## Context
He writes that "today's frontier LLM research is not about building animals" but about summoning ghosts, a fundamentally different kind of point in the space of possible intelligences ([[blog-2025-10-01-animals-vs-ghosts]] §intro). Ghosts, in his description, are muddled by humanity and thoroughly engineered by it: imperfect replicas, a statistical distillation of humanity's documents with some sprinkle on top ([[blog-2025-10-01-animals-vs-ghosts]] §intro).

He leaves the outcome open. It seems possible to him that ghosts can be finetuned more and more toward animals, a matter of initialization rather than fundamental incompatibility, and it is also quite possible that they diverge further and stay un-animal-like while still very helpful and world-altering ([[blog-2025-10-01-animals-vs-ghosts]] §intro). He offers "It's possible that ghosts:animals :: planes:birds." ([[blog-2025-10-01-animals-vs-ghosts]] §intro). He still thinks it worth being inspired by animals, since LLM agents are algorithmically missing several powerful ideas that could be adapted from animal intelligence ([[blog-2025-10-01-animals-vs-ghosts]] §intro). To someone who said ghosts are scary, he answers not necessarily, citing Casper, his childhood favourite ([[blog-2025-10-01-animals-vs-ghosts]] §Appendix).

**Note:** the `§intro` text cited here was also posted on X the same day, word for word; that post is a second location of the same words, not independent evidence, and has no Appendix ([[x-2025-10-01-sutton-podcast]] #1; [[blog-2025-10-01-animals-vs-ghosts]] §intro).

**In the Dwarkesh interview (published 2025-10-17).** Referring to the post, he says "In my post, I said we're not building animals.": we are building ghosts or spirits, because training is imitation of humans and their internet data rather than evolution, giving fully digital entities that mimic humans and start from a different point in the space of intelligences ([[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @08:49, @09:25). He says it is possible to make them a bit more animal-like over time and that we should ([[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @09:25). He is very careful with animal analogies, since animals came from a very different optimisation process, and calls himself practically minded, with a hard hat on, rather than setting out to build animals ([[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @08:14, @11:55, @12:28).
