# Hiring should test building and securing a big project with agents, not puzzles
> "if you're giving out puzzles to solve, then this is still the old paradigm" — [[talk-2026-04-29-sequoia-ascent-2026]] @18:34

## Context
He says most people have still not refactored their hiring process for agentic-engineer capability ([[talk-2026-04-29-sequoia-ascent-2026]] @18:34). Hiring, he says, has to look like giving someone a really big project, say a Twitter clone for agents, made really good and really secure, with agents simulating activity on it; he would then set ten Codex agents, as the garbled caption probably reads, to try to break the deployed site, and it should hold ([[talk-2026-04-29-sequoia-ascent-2026]] @18:34, @19:06). He hedges the format, "maybe it looks like that", and says watching people build bigger projects and use the tooling is what he would look at for the most part ([[talk-2026-04-29-sequoia-ascent-2026]] @19:06).
