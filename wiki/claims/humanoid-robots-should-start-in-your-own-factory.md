# Humanoid robots should start with their maker as first customer, then go to businesses, with consumers last
> "I think the best customer is yourself first" — [[talk-2024-09-05-no-priors-ep-80]] @08:48

## Context
He thinks household uses like laundry will come late, and that consumers are not the right place to start, because of legal liability and because the robots are not yet perfect ([[talk-2024-09-05-no-priors-ep-80]] @08:15, @08:48). The first customer is yourself, incubating the robot in your own factory on material handling; then business customers with large warehouses; then consumers ([[talk-2024-09-05-no-priors-ep-80]] @08:48, @09:21). He does expect consumer robots and an ecosystem building on those platforms, and says he will probably buy a Unitree robot ([[talk-2024-09-05-no-priors-ep-80]] @09:21). He expects deployment to be gradual: teleoperation first, then people supervising robots ([[talk-2024-09-05-no-priors-ep-80]] @13:45, @14:17).
