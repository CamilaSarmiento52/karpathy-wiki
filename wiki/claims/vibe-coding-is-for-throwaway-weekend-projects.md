# Vibe coding is fine for throwaway weekend projects
> "It's not too bad for throwaway weekend projects" — [[x-2025-02-02-vibe-coding]]

## Context
He gave this as the setting where the practice is acceptable, in the post that coined it, alongside saying the code grows beyond his comprehension and that it is not really coding ([[x-2025-02-02-vibe-coding]]). The post does not say weekend projects are the only valid use ([[x-2025-02-02-vibe-coding]]).

## Related claims
- Extended by [[vibe-coding-will-terraform-software]]: in his 2025-12-19 review he adds professional uses, such as nanochat's tokenizer ([[blog-2025-12-19-year-in-review-2025]] §5). **Note:** the two can both hold; the later source adds examples rather than reversing this one ([[x-2025-02-02-vibe-coding]], [[blog-2025-12-19-year-in-review-2025]]).
- Extended by [[deploying-a-vibe-coded-app-is-harder-than-writing-it]]: in the MenuGen post (2025-04-27) he takes a vibe coded app past the local demo to a deployed, paid product and finds the hard part is outside the code, a painful slog of services and settings ([[blog-2025-04-27-vibe-coding-menugen]] §intro ¶TLDR). Both hold: this claim is about where the practice is acceptable ([[x-2025-02-02-vibe-coding]]).
- Supported by [[vibe-coding-full-web-apps-is-not-yet-for-anything-important]]: the same post says vibe coding full web apps is not yet a good idea for anything of actual importance, again keeping it to low-stakes work ([[blog-2025-04-27-vibe-coding-menugen]] §intro ¶For now; [[x-2025-02-02-vibe-coding]]).
