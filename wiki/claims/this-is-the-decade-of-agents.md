# This is the decade of agents, not the year
> "this is the decade of agents" — [[youtube-2025-06-19-software-is-changing]] @27:09

## Context
He says claims that 2025 is "the year of agents" concern him ([[youtube-2025-06-19-software-is-changing]] @27:09). His evidence is self-driving: a perfect Waymo demo drive in 2013, and twelve years later the problem still needs teleoperation and humans in the loop ([[youtube-2025-06-19-software-is-changing]] @26:01, @26:35, @27:09).
