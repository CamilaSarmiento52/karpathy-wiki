# This is the decade of agents, not the year
> "this is the decade of agents" — [[youtube-2025-06-19-software-is-changing]] @27:09

## Context
He says claims that 2025 is "the year of agents" concern him ([[youtube-2025-06-19-software-is-changing]] @27:09). His evidence is self-driving: a perfect Waymo demo drive in 2013, and twelve years later the problem still needs teleoperation and humans in the loop ([[youtube-2025-06-19-software-is-changing]] @26:01, @26:35, @27:09). He concludes that software is tricky in the same way, so agents need humans in the loop and careful work ([[youtube-2025-06-19-software-is-changing]] @27:44).

**Restated with reasons (Dwarkesh, published 2025-10-17).** Asked why this is the decade and not the year of agents, he says the phrase "described as the decade of agents" was his reaction to someone's year-of-agents claim, which he felt was over-prediction in the industry ([[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @00:48). He uses very early agents daily, Claude and Codex, and calls them extremely impressive, but says we will be working with these things for a decade ([[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @01:25). He thinks of an agent as something like an employee or intern; today you would not hire Claude or Codex for that because they lack enough intelligence, are not multimodal enough, cannot do computer use and have no continual learning, and he says it will take about a decade to work through those issues ([[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @01:25, @01:59). Asked why a decade, he says it is intuition from about 15 years of watching predictions and how they turned out: the problems feel tractable but difficult, and averaged out it feels like a decade ([[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @02:32, @03:14). He says Claude Code and Codex still feel like an elementary-grade student, savant kids with perfect memory who don't really know what they are doing ([[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @1:43:07). He sounds pessimistic, he says, only in reaction to fundraising and attention on his Twitter timeline; overall he is very bullish and thinks it tractable ([[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @1:55:33).
