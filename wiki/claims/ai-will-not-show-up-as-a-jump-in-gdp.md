# He expects AI to diffuse into the same GDP exponential rather than produce a discrete jump in growth
> "I don't think that we will have this discrete change." — [[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @1:28:49

## Context
He says we are already in an intelligence explosion and have been for decades: the GDP curve is an exponential weighted sum over gradual automation since the Industrial Revolution ([[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @1:22:43). He looked for computers and mobile phones in GDP and could not find them, because technologies diffuse slowly and average into the same exponential, and he has convinced himself that the idea of finding AI there is false ([[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @1:23:57, @1:25:44). Asked by Dwarkesh whether growth stays on the roughly 2% trajectory, he answers that his expectation is that it stays in the same pattern ([[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @1:26:19). Against Dwarkesh's argument that AGI is labour itself, he says computing and self-driving already were labour and it was business as usual, while saying he is "pretty willing to be convinced" ([[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @1:27:00, @1:27:37). He rejects the assumption of "God in a box": AI will do some things, fail at others and be put into society gradually ([[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @1:28:11). The case for a jump, billions of AI minds and the population-growth argument, is Dwarkesh's ([[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @1:27:00, @1:32:06).
