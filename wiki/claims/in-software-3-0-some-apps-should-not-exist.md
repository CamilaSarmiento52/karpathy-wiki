# In the Software 3.0 version, an app like his MenuGen should not exist
> "that app shouldn't exist" — [[talk-2026-04-29-sequoia-ascent-2026]] @05:59

## Context
MenuGen, he explains, takes a photo of a restaurant menu, OCRs the titles, generates a picture for each with an image generator and re-renders the menu; it runs on Vercel ([[talk-2026-04-29-sequoia-ascent-2026]] @04:54, @05:26). The Software 3.0 version he saw, which blew his mind, gives the photo to Gemini and asks Nano Banana to overlay the dishes, returning his menu with the dishes rendered into the pixels ([[talk-2026-04-29-sequoia-ascent-2026]] @05:26). So his MenuGen is "spurious", working in the old paradigm: the neural network does more and more of the work, the input and output are images, and no app is needed in between ([[talk-2026-04-29-sequoia-ascent-2026]] @05:59). People should not think of AI only as a speed-up of what exists; new things are available now, such as LLM-compiled knowledge bases that no code could have produced before ([[talk-2026-04-29-sequoia-ascent-2026]] @05:59, @06:32, @07:04). He almost thinks those new possibilities are more exciting than the speed-ups ([[talk-2026-04-29-sequoia-ascent-2026]] @07:04, @07:35).
