# He thinks longer context windows alone will not scale to long-running multimodal tasks, and new ideas such as test-time training are needed
> "the context window is a finite and precious resource" — [[youtube-2025-02-05-deep-dive-into-llms]] @3:14:13

## Context
Among future directions he raises test-time training: once trained, the parameters are fixed, and the only learning a deployed model does is in-context learning in its context window, which he contrasts with humans, who learn from what they do, for instance during sleep ([[youtube-2025-02-05-deep-dive-into-llms]] @3:13:05, @3:13:39). He thinks more ideas need exploring because long-running multimodal tasks with video will push token windows far beyond hundreds of thousands of tokens, and making the window longer, the only trick available now, will not by itself scale ([[youtube-2025-02-05-deep-dive-into-llms]] @3:14:13, @3:14:46). The view is hedged with "I think" ([[youtube-2025-02-05-deep-dive-into-llms]] @3:14:13).
