# He thinks a gradual loss of understanding and control is the most likely outcome
> "there will be a gradual loss of understanding." — [[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @1:19:39

## Context
Describing superintelligence as an extrapolation of automation that will look extremely foreign, he says the scenarios that make him nervous involve a gradual loss of control and understanding, which he thinks the most likely outcome: this will be layered everywhere and fewer and fewer people will understand it ([[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @1:19:39). Pushed that understanding and control differ, he calls it a good pushback and says he expects the loss of both ([[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @1:20:53). He flags that this is territory where he does not know what it looks like; if he wrote sci-fi, it would be not one entity taking over but multiple competing entities becoming more autonomous, some going rogue and others fighting them off ([[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @1:20:53). The loss of control, in this picture, comes from their competition, not from their being smarter than us; they may act on behalf of people, yet society may lose control of the outcomes it wants ([[talk-2025-10-17-dwarkesh-ghosts-not-animals]] @1:21:31, @1:22:07).
