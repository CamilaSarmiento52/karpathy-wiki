# In 2023 he recommended LLMs for low-stakes uses, with human oversight, as copilots rather than autonomous agents
> "think co-pilots, instead of completely autonomous agents" — [[talk-2023-05-25-state-of-gpt]] @39:57

## Context
Closing State of GPT, he lists limitations: models may be biased, fabricate and hallucinate, make reasoning errors, struggle with whole classes of applications, have a knowledge cutoff around September 2021, and be open to attacks such as prompt injection, jailbreaks and data poisoning ([[talk-2023-05-25-state-of-gpt]] @39:24). So his recommendation is low-stakes applications, always combined with human oversight, using models as a source of inspiration and suggestions, because it is not clear the models are there yet ([[talk-2023-05-25-state-of-gpt]] @39:24, @39:57). Of AutoGPT, he finds it inspirationally interesting but does not think it works very well and would not advise using it in practical applications ([[talk-2023-05-25-state-of-gpt]] @29:28, @30:02).
