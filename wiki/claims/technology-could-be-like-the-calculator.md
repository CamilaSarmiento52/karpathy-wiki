# Technology could be like the calculator, at least as an ideal to approach
> "It could be like the calculator." — [[blog-2024-09-08-i-love-calculator]] §intro

## Context
After listing everything the calculator does not ask of its owner and the CO2 monitor that demanded an account, an app and precise location, he says it is up to us, as consumers and developers, to remember what technology could be: like the calculator ([[blog-2024-09-08-i-love-calculator]] §intro). He hedges it at once: maybe not in a concrete, practical sense for all products and services, but as a kind of platonic ideal one can approach along various dimensions ([[blog-2024-09-08-i-love-calculator]] §intro). What he means by the calculator is a fully self-contained, owned, private tool with an almost zero dependency footprint ([[blog-2024-09-08-i-love-calculator]] §intro).
